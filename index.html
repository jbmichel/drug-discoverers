What is in here

PathWhat it is
discoverers.pyThe 120-person roster. Source of truth; regenerates the CSV and Markdown views.
sources.mdSource manifest for the 61 Grade A people — 282 URLs, graded by confidence.
dossier/questions.yamlThe question bank. Each card names a persona and a source.
dossier/build_dossier.pyRenders an indication dossier spine from the bank.
ingest/The crawler. Untested against the live web — see its README.

Two caveats worth carrying. The 282 source URLs came from search results and none has been fetched to confirm it resolves — sources/check_links.py verifies them all in one pass. And the question bank's personas are reconstructions from public writing, not the people; every card cites a source so the attribution can be checked.
