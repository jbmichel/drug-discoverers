FTD-GRN: progranulin haploinsufficiency

First end-to-end run of the persona question bank through the rare-disease dive. Five kill gates worked in order; none scored zero, so the dive completed.

Superseded in part. A prior orchestrated run exists at rare-disease-program-verdict → runs/FTD-GRN-2026-08 (waves 1–2, 201 gated evidence rows, 0.78 measured). It is ahead of this dive on retrieval, provenance and scope. The comparison at the bottom is the more useful half of this page.

Do not enter on target validation. The target is validated and that is no longer the question.

The naive form of this program — raise progranulin, slow the disease — has been tested to completion and failed. Latozinemab's 96-week Phase 3 NCT04374136 missed its clinical co-primary while hitting its biomarker co-primary, raising plasma progranulin 165% over placebo at week 96, with no treatment-related effect on any secondary including fluid biomarkers and volumetric MRI. Alector cut 49% of staff and discontinued the extension; GSK leaves the alliance at the start of 2027.

That is a clean, well-powered, interpretable failure — the most valuable fact available about this indication. It does not say progranulin is the wrong target. It says this route, at this magnitude, in this compartment, does not change the disease. Three companies are already asking the narrower question with direct CNS delivery. Entry now is a fast-follow, and is only justified on an explicit delivery-or-durability thesis.

The gates

1

Causal human biology pass, strongly

Plenge; Hobbs. A genuine human allelic series. Heterozygous loss of function causes FTLD with TDP-43 inclusions; homozygous loss causes a different disease, neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis type 11 OMIM #614706. Direction of effect is unambiguous. Two refinements cut against treating it as a simple dose curve: homozygotes lack the TDP-43 inclusions that define the heterozygous disease, and the homozygous phenotype is itself split between juvenile CLN11 and a post-50 FTD-parkinsonism presentation.

Hobbs's outlier question is half-answered. The biallelic extreme is described; the other tail — heterozygotes who convert late or not at all, across an onset range of 40 to 85 — is where modifiers must live, and no systematic study of it surfaced. GENFI and ALLFTD already hold those cohorts.

2

Precedent pass — the gate that matters

Lowe; Edwards/Bountra. Lowe's question is whether we know why it failed rather than that it failed. Here we do, because the trial carried a biomarker co-primary alongside the clinical one, which makes the failure interpretable rather than ambiguous.

The pharmacology worked. In healthy volunteers a single infusion tripled plasma progranulin and doubled CSF progranulin; in cynomolgus monkeys both rose two- to three-fold. So this is not a drug that failed to reach the brain. It reached the CSF, roughly doubled progranulin there, and the disease did not respond on any measure. Two hypotheses survive: insufficient magnitude, or the compartment problem below.

Hypothesis — not retrieved from any source Sortilin is a principal route for progranulin uptake into cells and delivery to the lysosome, where its known functions are exercised. Blocking sortilin raises measured extracellular progranulin partly by preventing that uptake. If so, the biomarker that was hit and the effect that was wanted are in tension, and plasma progranulin is a target-engagement marker for sortilin blockade rather than a surrogate for intracellular sufficiency. Falsifiable: did intracellular or lysosomal progranulin rise under latozinemab, and does sortilin-independent uptake carry enough flux to compensate? This decides whether the failure reads across to NCT06705192, which is also sortilin-directed.
3

Tractability and delivery pass

Hopkins; Cullis. Three modalities have raised progranulin in the human CNS: AVB-101 (AAV, intrathalamic; 12 patients dosed, fourth cohort from March 2026), PBFT02 (AAV, intra-cisterna magna; CSF progranulin 22.8 ng/mL at 12 months on dose 1), and DNL593 (BBB transport vehicle; 40 enrolled, readout end 2026). The delivery question is answered for the measurable compartment and open for distribution — the same gap Gate 2 opened, arriving from the other side.

4

Model predictive validity weak — weakest gate

Scannell. The preclinical package predicted target engagement accurately and clinical outcome not at all. A model that forecasts your biomarker but not your endpoint generates confidence without generating information.

The reason is visible in the model ecosystem: the genotype matching the human disease is Grn+/-, reported to be nearly phenotype-free, so the field works in Grn−/− — which is the genotype of CLN11, a disease Gate 1 says is pathologically distinct. The workhorse model may be a model of the sibling disease. No published concordance analysis between any GRN model and clinical outcome surfaced.

5

Endpoints weak pass

Pangalos. An accepted endpoint exists — CDR plus NACC FTLD-SB — and a 96-week multi-continent Phase 3 could not move it. NfL is better characterised in this population than in most: GRN carriers run an order of magnitude above non-carriers a decade before onset, and the Bluefield surveillance programme is measuring it quarterly in 335+ carriers to qualify it for Phase 3. That is a qualification effort in progress, not a qualified endpoint.

Passage Bio's interim data claim movement on exactly what latozinemab could not move — 64% less whole-brain atrophy and stabilised plasma NfL versus ALLFTD natural history. But those are external comparisons in a small early cohort selected for early disease, and FDA has told them a randomised registrational trial is required.

What survived the comparison

  • Scannell's gate is missing from the specialist set. 201 ledger rows, two mention mouse models. No contract asks whether the model predicts clinical outcome. Structural gap, not a retrieval gap — deeper searching cannot surface it.
  • Sortilin read-across. Both facts are in the ledger; no row connects them. Offered as hypothesis.
  • Stranded datasets, not just assets. Alector holds unpublished Phase 3 CSF and vMRI data in this exact genotype and has stopped needing it.

The threshold row is the sharpest rebuke. This memo proposed "assemble the dose-response curve nobody has published" as an opportunity. The prior run had the number a week earlier. That is what an unanchored dive does — it mistakes its own retrieval limit for a gap in the field. There was also a scope error: the prior run froze the stratum as symptomatic FTD-GRN, excluding CLN11 and presymptomatic carriers, which Gates 1 and 5 above both lean on.

Recommendation

Do not run the question bank as a parallel dive. Wire it into O.build_brief() as criteria that generate specialist contracts: add a MODEL_VALIDITY specialist carrying Scannell's question, extend UNLOCK_SCOUT to stranded datasets, and feed the kill-gate ordering into dispatch order — which addresses the prior run's own finding that an option-set choice made before wave 1 returns can rest on a premise wave 1 destroys.
